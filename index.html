PROJECT 01 · ENERGY SYSTEMS
German Energy Grid & Carbon Intensity Forecasting
End-to-end multi-horizon predictive pipeline · Benchmarking XGBoost vs. Foundation Models
GitHub ↗
Challenge

Grid operators require precise 24-hour demand and carbon intensity forecasts to shift heavy industrial loads toward renewable peaks. Standard autoregressive models suffer from error compounding over extended horizons. This forces operators to maintain expensive spinning reserve capacity to account for prediction uncertainty.

Solution

Engineered an automated pipeline ingesting 80,000 records from multiple federal APIs. I designed physical composite features to capture diurnal cycles and grid load behaviors directly. I then executed a comparative evaluation between optimized XGBoost configurations and zero-shot deployment of Amazon Chronos-T5.

Impact

Demonstrated that foundation models sustain predictive accuracy well beyond the 18-hour mark where classical models typically degrade. For an industrial stakeholder, this reliability directly translates into reduced backup power costs and highly effective load scheduling. The core architecture is modular and easily adapts to new geographic zones.

PROJECT 02 · DIGITAL TWINS
Virtual Soft-Sensor for Turbine Emissions Monitoring
Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) architecture · Real-time operational validation
GitHub ↗
Challenge

Physical exhaust probes tracking hazardous gases like CO and NOx operate under aggressive thermal conditions exceeding 600°C. Frequent sensor drift and hardware degradation lead to costly operational blind spots. Relying on traditional uncalibrated data models creates severe regulatory compliance risks during transient plant operations.

Solution

Developed a custom deep learning soft-sensor incorporating a thermodynamic loss penalty alongside standard data error metrics:

ℒ = ℒdata + λ · ℒphysics

This formulation enforces strict compliance with mass and energy balances. I implemented Monte Carlo Dropout during inference to provide real-time statistical confidence bounds alongside every point prediction.

Impact

Delivered an accurate predictive engine that infers emissions values using existing operational telemetry. This approach eliminates the immediate need for auxiliary instrumentation while ensuring compliance. The model feeds directly into an interactive dashboard designed for plant managers to monitor real-time process health.
