01 Manufacturing · Data Engineering · Analytics

Enterprise Manufacturing Intelligence Platform

A six-site manufacturing intelligence platform that brings production, downtime, energy, quality, maintenance, and equipment telemetry into one decision layer, combining OEE and loss analysis with forecasting, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance.

Engineering Problem

Multi-site operations generate millions of records across systems built for different purposes, time scales, and KPI definitions. The challenge was to reconcile them into a trusted enterprise model where performance, losses, energy use, reliability, and data quality could be compared consistently across sites and production lines.

Approach

Built a governed Python and PostgreSQL data platform with Power BI reporting and AWS-ready infrastructure. The platform integrates enterprise operations with industrial reference data, adds automated data-quality controls, and extends historical performance analysis into forecasting, anomaly detection, and early-warning analytics.

Python · PostgreSQL · Power BI · AWS · Terraform

02 Wastewater · Multiphase CFD

Multiphase Aeration Hydrodynamics & Treatment Performance

A multiphase CFD study showing how diffuser placement changes circulation, gas distribution, oxygen transfer, dissolved oxygen, and biodegradable substrate removal, even when total airflow is held constant.

Engineering Problem

The same aeration rate does not guarantee the same treatment conditions. Diffuser layout can create uneven circulation, oxygen-transfer zones, and low-DO regions that directly affect biological treatment performance.

Approach

Compared distributed, central, and lateral diffuser layouts under identical tank geometry, hydraulics, and total airflow. Time-averaged multiphase aeration fields were coupled with a reduced ASM1 formulation to evaluate dissolved oxygen and biodegradable substrate reduction.

OpenFOAM · Multiphase Flow · Aeration · ParaView

03 Atmospheric CFD · Pollutant Transport

Atmospheric Pollutant Dispersion

Site-resolved CFD investigation of atmospheric pollutant dispersion under varying meteorological conditions across realistic terrain, buildings and industrial structures.

Engineering Problem

Wind direction, atmospheric flow and building wakes influence plume transport, recirculation, dilution and local pollutant concentration.

Approach

Combined terrain and building geometry with atmospheric boundary-layer simulations, Eulerian concentration fields and Lagrangian particle transport.

OpenFOAM · RANS / LES · QGIS · ParaView · Blender

04 Energy Systems · Time-Series Forecasting

German Grid Demand & Carbon Intensity Forecasting

A multi-horizon forecasting study of the German electricity system, using power-system and weather data to forecast grid demand, renewable generation, and carbon intensity across a 24-hour horizon.

Forecasting Problem

Electricity demand, renewable generation, and carbon intensity evolve differently with weather, generation mix, and recurring system behavior. The challenge was to compare forecasting approaches consistently while preserving temporal causality and understanding how accuracy changes as the prediction horizon extends.

Approach

Built an end-to-end hourly forecasting pipeline from OPSD, SMARD, and Meteostat data, then compared seasonal baselines, SARIMA, recursive XGBoost, and zero-shot Chronos, TimesFM, and Moirai across nine targets.

Python · XGBoost · Chronos · TimesFM · Moirai

05 Process Engineering · Emissions · ML

Virtual Soft-Sensor for Turbine Emissions Monitoring

A physics-constrained soft sensor for estimating CO and NOx from operating telemetry when direct exhaust measurements become unavailable or unreliable.

Engineering Problem

Calibration drift, analyzer maintenance, and measurement interruptions can create blind spots in emissions monitoring while the turbine continues operating.

Approach

Developed an uncertainty-aware neural soft sensor combining physics-constrained modelling, thermodynamically motivated features, and conformal prediction to estimate emissions from existing operational variables while quantifying prediction uncertainty.

Python · PyTorch · Thermodynamics · Streamlit
